# ChanGu Privacy Notice Template

This document is a deployment template and requires legal review before publication.

ChanGu may process account details, service requests, order and payment status, delivery and ride location data where required for an active service, notifications, AI conversations, personalization preferences, recommendation activity, and operational logs.

ChanGu uses this information to authenticate users, provide Food, Shop, Parcel, Ride, Siren, notification, payment, support, security, and recommendation features. Payment credentials should be handled by the approved payment provider; ChanGu should retain only permitted transaction references and statuses.

AI conversations and personalization are user-scoped. Users can disable personalization, disable AI memory, disable recommendations, and clear stored preference/activity data through the application controls. Service records required for transactions, safety, fraud prevention, accounting, or legal compliance may require separate retention rules.

Location is shared only where required for an active delivery, ride, or Siren workflow and is restricted by backend authorization. ChanGu does not claim that Siren replaces official emergency services.

Before publication, the owner and legal counsel must add the legal entity, contact address, jurisdiction, retention periods, user rights, deletion process, subprocessors, cross-border transfer terms, and effective date.
