# ChanGu Terms Template

This document is a deployment template and requires legal review before publication.

ChanGu provides platform coordination for Food, Shop, Parcel, Ride, and Siren service requests. Availability, delivery times, provider acceptance, fares, payment status, cancellation eligibility, and refunds are governed by the backend state and the applicable service rules.

Users must provide accurate account, address, contact, and service information; protect their credentials; avoid prohibited or unlawful use; and not misuse APIs, locations, payments, reviews, notifications, or AI tools.

Food and Shop orders, Parcel deliveries, Rides, and Siren requests may have different cancellation, payment, refund, provider, driver, and availability rules. AI responses are informational and may be inaccurate; users should verify important information. AI is not an emergency dispatcher or a replacement for official emergency services.

Vendors, drivers, and emergency providers are responsible for accurate business/profile information, lawful operations, valid credentials, and following backend workflow and safety rules. Admin actions remain subject to authorization and audit controls.

Before publication, the owner and legal counsel must add the legal entity, jurisdiction, payment/refund terms, user responsibilities, acceptable-use rules, dispute process, liability language, support contact, effective date, and service-specific policies.
